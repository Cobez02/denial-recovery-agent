## Recitals

A. Covered Entity is a health care provider and a covered entity under HIPAA.

B. Recipient owns and develops Plumbline, denial-recovery software (the "Software").

C. Covered Entity wishes to disclose a limited data set to Recipient for the purpose described in Section 1,
which is part of Covered Entity's health care operations, and the parties enter into this Agreement as
required by 45 C.F.R. § 164.514(e).

The parties therefore agree as follows.

## 1. Purpose

Covered Entity discloses the Limited Data Set (Section 2) to Recipient solely to evaluate the accuracy of
the Software's classification of Covered Entity's denied claims and to report the results to Covered
Entity, as part of Covered Entity's health care operations (the "Purpose"). The Limited Data Set may not be
used to prepare, correct or submit any claim or appeal.

## 2. Limited Data Set

2.1 **Permitted content.** The limited data set (the "Limited Data Set") may include dates (including dates
of service, submission, payment and denial), town or city, state and ZIP code, payer identifiers,
procedure and diagnosis codes, modifiers, units, charges, paid amounts, denial reason and remark codes,
and text of payer correspondence or clinical notes from which every identifier listed in Section 2.2 has
been removed.

2.2 **Excluded identifiers.** The Limited Data Set excludes the following direct identifiers of the
individual or of relatives, employers, or household members of the individual (45 C.F.R.
§ 164.514(e)(2)): names; postal address information, other than town or city, State, and zip code;
telephone numbers; fax numbers; electronic mail addresses; social security numbers; medical record
numbers; health plan beneficiary numbers; account numbers; certificate/license numbers; vehicle
identifiers and serial numbers, including license plate numbers; device identifiers and serial numbers;
Web Universal Resource Locators (URLs); Internet Protocol (IP) address numbers; biometric identifiers,
including finger and voice prints; and full face photographic images and any comparable images.

2.3 **Substitute codes.** Covered Entity replaces claim numbers and patient account numbers with codes that
are not derived from them, keeps the key, and does not disclose the key to Recipient. Covered Entity
reviews free text before disclosure.

2.4 **Form and processing.** Covered Entity delivers the Limited Data Set as a spreadsheet of the
permitted fields through the secure transfer service. Recipient processes it only in a separate
installation of the Software with its own database, runs only the Software's classification rules on it,
creates no corrected claims, appeals or other proposals from it, and never combines it with data held
under the Business Associate Agreement between the parties.

## 3. Permitted Users

Only Connor Betz may use or receive the Limited Data Set, and only on the dedicated, encrypted workstation
account described in Recipient's security policies.

## 4. Obligations of Recipient

Recipient shall:

(a) not use or further disclose the information other than as permitted by this Agreement or as otherwise
required by law;

(b) use appropriate safeguards to prevent use or disclosure of the information other than as provided for
by this Agreement;

(c) report to Covered Entity any use or disclosure of the information not provided for by this Agreement of
which it becomes aware, within five (5) business days;

(d) ensure that any agents to whom it provides the Limited Data Set agree to the same restrictions and
conditions that apply to Recipient with respect to such information;

(e) not identify the information or contact the individuals;

(f) use the Limited Data Set only for the Purpose, and not use or disclose it in any manner that would
violate Subpart E of 45 C.F.R. Part 164 if done by Covered Entity;

(g) process the Limited Data Set only on Recipient's own workstation, without sending any of it to a
language model provider or any other third party;

(h) protect the Limited Data Set with the safeguards in Recipient's security policies, including disk
encryption, a dedicated operating-system account, no cloud synchronization and no transfer by e-mail;

(i) publish nothing derived from the Limited Data Set other than aggregate results approved in writing by
Covered Entity;

(j) comply with Subpart C of 45 C.F.R. Part 164 with respect to the Limited Data Set in electronic form;

(k) ensure, in accordance with 45 C.F.R. § 164.308(b)(2), that any subcontractor that creates, receives,
maintains or transmits the Limited Data Set on Recipient's behalf agrees in writing to comply with
Subpart C of 45 C.F.R. Part 164; and

(l) report to Covered Entity any security incident involving the Limited Data Set of which it becomes
aware, including a breach of unsecured protected health information as required by 45 C.F.R. § 164.410,
within five (5) business days.

## 5. Term and Termination

5.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and ends on the earlier of completion of the
Purpose and ninety (90) days after the Effective Date, unless extended in writing.

5.2 **Termination.** Covered Entity may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if Recipient
breaches it.

5.3 **Return and destruction.** Within thirty (30) days after this Agreement ends, Recipient shall destroy
the Limited Data Set and all copies, including the separate installation's database, and shall deliver to
Covered Entity a signed certificate stating what was destroyed, by what method and on what date.

## 6. General

6.1 **Governing law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the state in which Covered Entity's
principal office is located, to the extent not preempted by federal law.

6.2 **Relationship to the Business Associate Agreement.** The Business Associate Agreement between the
parties also applies to the Limited Data Set, which Recipient receives as Covered Entity's business
associate; if the two agreements conflict, the more protective term applies. This Agreement may be
amended only in writing signed by both parties.

6.3 **Counterparts; electronic signatures.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by
electronic signature, each of which is deemed an original.
