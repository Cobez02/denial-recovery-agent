## Pilot Terms

### 1. Definitions

"BAA" means the Business Associate Agreement between the parties signed with this Agreement.

"Backlog Claim" means a Pilot Claim whose denial is dated before the first day of the Live Phase.

"Business Day" means Monday through Friday, excluding federal holidays.

"Claims in Scope" means the denied claims that appear in remittance files dated within the earliest and
latest remittance (denial) dates stated on the Cover Page, whatever their dates of service.

"Clinic Data" means all data the Clinic provides under this Agreement and everything the Software
produces from it for the Clinic.

"Export Package" means the file the Software produces for one work item approved by a Clinic reviewer.

"Live Phase" and "Shadow Phase" have the meanings given in Section 3.

"Pilot Claim" means a denied claim for which a Clinic reviewer approved an Export Package during the Live
Phase and the Clinic submitted the corrected claim or appeal.

"Recovered Amount" means the amount a payer paid on a Pilot Claim as a result of the corrected claim or
appeal, less the amount the payer had already paid on that claim. Amounts a payer takes back are handled
under Section 8.5.

"Software" means Plumbline, the denial-recovery software owned by BTP.

### 2. Services

2.1 **What BTP provides.** BTP operates the Software for the Clinic. The Software reads
the Clinic's electronic remittance advice (X12 835) files and claim data, identifies denied claims,
classifies each denial, proposes a fix (a corrected claim, an appeal, or a question for the Clinic),
drafts appeal letters using only the claim's facts and quoted excerpts from the clinical notes, lists the
work by deadline and dollar value, and records the outcome of each claim when later remittance files
arrive.

2.2 **What the Software does not do.** The Software does not submit anything to any payer or
clearinghouse and has no connection to either. It does not contact patients. It does not change a
procedure code, diagnosis code or modifier without a person's approval, and a work item with a coding
change cannot be approved until the reviewer attaches a quoted excerpt from the clinical note. The
Software checks only that an excerpt is attached; the reviewer, and the Clinic's coder or treating
provider under Section 4.1, confirm that it supports each change. The Software does not provide medical,
legal or coding advice and does not replace the Clinic's biller, coder or providers.

2.3 **Operation.** BTP operates the Software on its own workstation in the United States, within a
dedicated operating-system account used only for this work, on an encrypted disk, in accordance with
BTP's written security policies. The Clinic runs only the Software's anonymize and deanonymize
commands, on a Clinic computer, under Section 5(h); BTP does not operate them and does not receive
the Clinic's original files, the key files or restored Export Packages. The Software does not send
any information to a language model provider until the conditions in Exhibit B of the BAA are met
and the Clinic has received written notice.

2.4 **Possible overpayments.** If BTP notices a possible overpayment to the Clinic, such as a duplicate
payment or a payment made in error, it shall tell the Clinic in writing within five (5) Business Days,
identifying it by work item number or remittance reference and without patient names. The Clinic decides
whether to refund.

### 3. Pilot Phases

3.1 **Shadow Phase.** For approximately the first three (3) weeks after the Start Date, the Software works
denials the Clinic has already handled, and nothing it produces is sent anywhere. Each week the parties
compare the Software's denial category and proposed action with those of the Clinic Biller, and
BTP adjusts the Software's rules, prompts and templates in response. The Shadow Phase ends when the
targets in Exhibit A have been met in two consecutive weekly reviews and the Clinic agrees in writing,
which may be by e-mail, to begin the Live Phase.

3.2 **Live Phase.** For the remainder of the Pilot Period, approximately nine (9) weeks, the Clinic sends
new remittance files and unworked denials each week; the Software classifies, proposes and drafts; Clinic
reviewers approve, edit or reject each item; approved items are delivered as Export Packages; the Clinic
decides whether to submit each one and submits it itself; a Clinic reviewer records each submission in the
Software; and later remittance files record the outcome. Corrected claims are worked first, in order of
filing deadline, then dollar value; appeals proceed in parallel.

3.3 **Pilot Period and extension.** The Pilot Period begins on the Start Date and ends on the End of Pilot
Period date stated on the Cover Page, twelve (12) weeks after the Start Date. The parties may extend it
once, by up to eight (8) weeks, by a written agreement stating the new end date, which may be by e-mail;
the latest remittance date for Claims in Scope then moves to the new end date.

### 4. Decisions and Submissions

4.1 **The Clinic decides.** Clinic staff approve, edit or reject every proposed action. The Clinic's
certified coder or the treating provider confirms every coding change before the Clinic submits it.

4.2 **The Clinic submits.** The Clinic submits every corrected claim, appeal and response to a payer
through its own channels, under its own name and provider identifiers. Each Export Package states that it
was not submitted by the Software.

4.3 **Responsibility for submissions.** The Clinic is responsible for the accuracy and completeness of each
claim, corrected claim and appeal it submits, and for its compliance with payer requirements and
applicable law.

### 5. Clinic Responsibilities

The Clinic shall:

(a) provide the data described in Section 6 and Exhibit B on the schedule the parties agree;

(b) make the Clinic Contact and the Clinic Biller available for the Weekly Review;

(c) make review decisions in time for the payer deadlines the Software displays, checking a displayed
deadline against the payer's rule where the two can differ, such as a Medicare claim returned as
unprocessable, which is resubmitted as a new claim within twelve (12) months of the date of service;

(d) ensure that each person who reviews items in the Software has an individual account, never shared,
with an authenticator application enrolled, and tell BTP the same day when a person's access
should end;

(e) not enter patient names or other patient identifiers, including the Clinic's own claim numbers
for pseudonymized claims, in any field of the Software (reason fields are retained in its permanent
audit log);

(f) tell BTP promptly about any suspected security incident on the Clinic's side that may affect
the pilot;

(g) confirm each payer's filing, corrected-claim and appeal deadlines from its contracts or payer
manuals; and

(h) pseudonymize its files with the Software's anonymize command before each transfer and restore
each Export Package with the Software's deanonymize command before submitting it, both on a Clinic
computer and as Exhibit B describes; keep the key files, with backup copies, under the Clinic's
control and use only the newest; and never submit an Export Package whose name begins with
"PSEUDONYMIZED_".

### 6. Data in Scope; Minimum Necessary

6.1 **In scope:** (a) the remittance files for the Claims in Scope; (b) a report of denied claims only,
and of the claims the Clinic sent to replace them, with the fields listed in Exhibit B; (c) clinical
note excerpts, only for denied claims in scope, limited to the denied visit and, for a medical-necessity
denial, the initial examination or the latest re-examination; (d) the text of payer letters and portal
messages for those claims; and (e) each payer's filing, corrected-claim and appeal deadlines.

6.2 **Out of scope:** complete medical records, Social Security numbers, images of insurance cards or
identification documents, payment card or bank account information, and claims that were not denied
(other than as they appear in remittance files, and the claims the Clinic sent to replace denied claims).

6.3 **Use.** BTP uses Clinic Data only as permitted by the BAA.

### 7. Data Transfer and Reviewer Access

7.1 **Transfer.** Files move only through the Secure Transfer Method, which encrypts data in transit and at
rest. The parties shall not send Clinic Data by e-mail attachment, removable media, text message or chat,
and shall not share log-in credentials. If BTP provides the Secure Transfer Method, or the video service
for the Weekly Review, that service is BTP's subcontractor under Section 2.4 of the BAA, and its selection
on the Cover Page is the Clinic's written consent.

7.2 **Returns.** Export Packages and the hand-over copy described in Section 14 are delivered to the Clinic
through the Secure Transfer Method.

7.3 **Reviewer access.** Clinic reviewers use the Software by the Reviewer Access method selected on the
Cover Page. The Software is not accessible from the internet. A reviewer working in person uses the
Software only at BTP's workstation, in a session that BTP opens and attends, signs in with the reviewer's
own Software account, and signs out of the Software before leaving the workstation.

### 8. Fees

8.1 **No fee.** If "No fee" is selected on the Cover Page, the pilot is provided free of charge, each party
bears its own costs, and Sections 8.2 through 8.8 do not apply.

8.2 **Success Fee.** If "Success Fee" is selected, the Clinic shall pay BTP the Success Fee rate
stated on the Cover Page multiplied by each Recovered Amount on a Backlog Claim or on any Pilot Claim, as
selected. No fee is due for the Shadow Phase, for items the Clinic rejected or did not submit, or for
amounts paid by patients.

8.3 **Coding changes.** BTP shall not disable or bypass the Software's requirement that a reviewer attach
a quoted excerpt from the clinical note before a coding change is approved. No Success Fee is due on an
amount recovered through a coding change that the Clinic or a payer later finds unsupported by the
clinical documentation, and a fee already paid on that amount is credited or refunded as described in
Section 8.5.

8.4 **Statements.** Each month during the Pilot Period BTP sends the Clinic a statement listing, by work
item number, each payment date and Recovered Amount. Each Recovered Amount is computed from the
remittance files the Clinic provided, as the later payment less the amount the payer had already paid.
If the statement differs from the Clinic's payment posting, the Clinic's payment posting controls. Payment
is due thirty (30) days after the date of the statement.

8.5 **Take-backs.** BTP does not keep a Success Fee on any amount a payer takes back. If a payer takes
back all or part of a Recovered Amount, and the Clinic reports the take-back to BTP, identified by work
item number only, within twelve (12) months after the payer's payment, BTP credits the Success Fee on that
amount on the next statement or, if no further statement is due, refunds it within thirty (30) days after
the report.

8.6 **Tail and cap.** Recovered Amounts received up to ninety (90) days after the end of the Pilot Period on
corrections or appeals submitted during the Pilot Period are included. Within thirty (30) days after that
ninety-day period ends, the Clinic reports to BTP from its payment posting, identified by work item number
only, each such payment net of the amount the payer had already paid, and BTP's final statement is
prepared from that report. After the Pilot Period, Recovered Amounts and take-backs are taken only from
the Clinic's reports; BTP keeps no Clinic Data to prepare them. Total fees under this Agreement shall not
exceed the Success Fee cap stated on the Cover Page.

8.7 **Payments.** BTP does not receive payer payments. All payer payments are made to the Clinic.

8.8 **Applicable rules.** If either party's counsel advises in writing that the Success Fee may violate a
fee-splitting, billing-agent or Medicaid rule that applies to the Clinic, the Success Fee does not apply
to the affected payers or claims, and the No fee option applies to them.

### 9. No Guarantee

BTP does not guarantee that any denial will be overturned or that any amount will be recovered,
and does not warrant that the Software's classifications, deadlines or drafts are free of errors. Payers
decide claims, and the Clinic reviews every item before acting on it. Except as expressly stated in this
Agreement and the BAA, the Software and the services are provided "as is."

### 10. Ownership

10.1 **Clinic Data.** The Clinic owns the Clinic Data and may keep and use the work items, letters,
corrected claims and Export Packages produced for it after the pilot ends.

10.2 **Software.** BTP owns the Software, including its code, rules, prompts, templates and
documentation, and all improvements to them, including changes made in response to what the pilot shows.

10.3 **No Clinic Data in the Software.** Improvements to the Software contain no protected health
information or other Clinic Data, and test cases for them use synthetic data only. BTP may use
suggestions the Clinic makes about the Software without obligation.

### 11. Confidentiality

11.1 **Obligation.** Each party shall keep the other party's confidential information, including the
Clinic's payer contracts and fee schedules and BTP's Software and pricing, confidential, shall use
it only for the pilot, and shall disclose it only to persons who need it for the pilot and are bound by
confidentiality obligations at least as protective as these.

11.2 **Exclusions.** Confidential information does not include information that is or becomes public
through no fault of the recipient, that the recipient already knew without restriction, that the
recipient lawfully receives from a third party without restriction, or that the recipient develops
independently. A recipient may disclose confidential information when required by law, after notifying
the other party where permitted.

11.3 **Protected health information.** Protected health information is governed by the BAA, which controls
in the event of a conflict.

### 12. Results and Case Study

12.1 **Aggregate results.** BTP may use aggregate results of the pilot (counts, rates, review times
and dollar totals, without record-level data) to improve and describe the Software.

12.2 **Case study.** BTP may publish a case study of the pilot only as selected on the Cover Page.
Any case study contains no protected health information and no record-level data. BTP shall send
the final text to the Clinic for written approval before publication, and the Clinic may withdraw its
approval at any time before publication. Quotations from Clinic staff require their written consent.

12.3 **Accuracy.** Any public statement by either party about the pilot shall be accurate and not
misleading, and shall disclose any material connection between the parties as required by law.

### 13. Liability and Insurance

13.1 **Responsibility.** Each party is responsible for its own acts and omissions, its own personnel and its
own compliance with law. BTP is responsible for operating the Software, other than the commands the
Clinic runs under Section 5(h), and protecting Clinic Data as the BAA requires; the Clinic is
responsible for its decisions and submissions under Section 4.

13.2 **Limitation of liability.** This Section 13.2 applies to claims under this Agreement and under the
BAA. Each party's total liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the BAA shall not exceed
the greater of (a) the fees paid or payable under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months before the
event giving rise to the claim and (b) the General Liability Cap amount stated on the Cover Page. Neither
party is liable for lost profits or for indirect, special or consequential damages. BTP's reimbursement
obligation under Section 2.3(e) of the BAA, and the Clinic's reasonable, documented costs of responding
to a breach of unsecured protected health information (as defined in 45 C.F.R. § 164.402) caused by BTP
or its subcontractor, are direct damages, are not subject to the two preceding sentences, and are limited
to the cyber liability insurance limit stated on the Cover Page. This Section 13.2 does not limit fees
owed under Section 8 or liability for a party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct.

13.3 **Insurance.** Before any protected health information is shared, and until BTP delivers the
Certificate of Return and Destruction, BTP shall maintain technology errors-and-omissions insurance and
cyber liability insurance (covering privacy and network security liability, breach response costs and
regulatory defense) with at least the limits stated on the Cover Page, each claim and in the aggregate (a
single limit shared by both coverages must be at least the sum of the two amounts), and shall provide
certificates of insurance to the Clinic as Exhibit C. If either policy is written on a claims-made basis,
BTP shall maintain coverage, or purchase an extended reporting period, for at least two (2) years after
the Pilot Period ends. BTP shall notify the Clinic within five (5) Business Days if either policy is
cancelled or its limits fall below those on the Cover Page.

### 14. Term, Termination and Return of Data

14.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and ends at the end of the Pilot Period, unless
terminated earlier under this Section 14.

14.2 **Termination.** Either party may terminate this Agreement for any reason on thirty (30) days' written
notice. Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other party
materially breaches this Agreement or the BAA and does not cure the breach within thirty (30) days after
written notice, or if cure is not possible.

14.3 **Return and destruction.** When this Agreement ends, BTP shall stop processing Clinic Data, other
than the Clinic's reports under Sections 8.5 and 8.6, and, within thirty (30) days:

(a) run the Software's clinic purge, which deletes every uploaded file and Export Package the Software
stored, writes the Clinic's records into a single file (the "hand-over copy"), deletes those records
(remittance records remain without patient names or member identifiers until the database file is
destroyed under Section 14.3(c)), rewrites the database file so that deleted records do not remain
readable in it, and records the deletion in the Software's audit log;

(b) deliver the hand-over copy, which includes the open work items and their deadlines, to the Clinic
through the Secure Transfer Method, and delete it after the Clinic confirms receipt or declines it, as
Section 5.3(c) of the BAA describes, including its written reminder and its extension of this period;

(c) destroy all other copies of Clinic Data it holds, including source files, files on a Secure Transfer
Method BTP provides, downloaded copies, backups and the remaining database file; and

(d) deliver to the Clinic a signed Certificate of Return and Destruction in the form of Exhibit D.

14.4 **Open items.** With the hand-over copy, BTP shall provide a list of open items and their
deadlines so that the Clinic can complete them without the Software.

14.5 **Survival.** Sections 4.3, 8 (as to amounts accrued, Recovered Amounts received during the period in
Section 8.6, and credits and refunds under Sections 8.3 and 8.5), 9, 10, 11, 12, 13 and 14 survive the end
of this Agreement.

### 15. Contacts and Weekly Review

15.1 **Contacts.** The contacts named on the Cover Page act for each party in day-to-day matters. Each party
shall notify the other in writing of any change.

15.2 **Weekly Review.** The parties meet for thirty (30) minutes each week at the Weekly Review day, time and
format stated on the Cover Page. After each meeting BTP sends a short written summary that
identifies items by work item number only.

15.3 **Security incidents.** Security incidents are reported as required by the BAA, by telephone and in
writing to the privacy contacts.

### 16. General

16.1 **Governing law and venue.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the state in which the Clinic's
principal office is located, and any dispute shall be brought in the state or federal courts serving the
county in which that office is located.

16.2 **Independent contractors.** The parties are independent contractors. Neither party is the agent,
employee or partner of the other.

16.3 **Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written
consent.

16.4 **Notices.** Notices are given in writing to the contact for notices on the Cover Page. E-mail is
sufficient, except that a notice of termination must also be sent by courier or certified mail.

16.5 **Entire agreement; order of precedence.** This Agreement, its Exhibits and the BAA are the entire
agreement of the parties about the pilot. The BAA controls with respect to protected health information;
otherwise this Agreement controls.

16.6 **Amendments and waivers.** Amendments and waivers must be in writing and signed by both parties.
Electronic signatures are effective.

16.7 **Compliance with law.** Each party shall comply with the laws that apply to it in performing this
Agreement.

16.8 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions
remain in effect.

16.9 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original.

16.10 **Exclusion.** BTP represents that neither BTP nor any person it uses to perform this Agreement is
excluded, debarred or otherwise ineligible to participate in a federal health care program, and shall
notify the Clinic within two (2) Business Days if that changes.

## Exhibit A. Pilot Measures

**Shadow Phase targets**, measured at each Weekly Review:

| Measure | How it is counted | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Category agreement | The Software's denial category matches the Clinic Biller's | 80% or better |
| Action agreement | The Software's proposed action matches what worked, or the Clinic Biller confirms it would have worked | 70% or better |
| Usable drafts | Appeals and corrections the Clinic Biller would send with only minor edits | 70% or better |
| Missed recoveries | Recoverable denials the Software marked as requiring no action or did not turn into a work item | Each reviewed individually; none caused by a repeatable rule gap |
| Security | Incidents involving protected health information; protected health information found in logs | Zero |

**End-of-pilot review**, for information: first Software-assisted claim paid; number of denials processed
in both phases; category agreement; share of drafts approved with minor or no edits; overturn rate on
submitted corrections and appeals compared with the Clinic's historical rate; average review time per
item; dollars recovered that the Clinic would likely otherwise have written off; security incidents; and
whether the Clinic wishes to continue.
