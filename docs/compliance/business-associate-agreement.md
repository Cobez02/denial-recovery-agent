## Recitals

A. Covered Entity is a health care provider and a covered entity under HIPAA.

B. Business Associate provides denial-recovery services to Covered Entity under the pilot agreement
between the parties (the "Pilot Agreement") using Plumbline, denial-recovery software owned by
Business Associate (the "Software"). The Software reads Covered Entity's electronic remittance advice
(X12 835) files and claim data, identifies and classifies denied claims, proposes corrected claims,
appeals or questions for Covered Entity's staff, drafts appeal letters, and tracks the outcome of each
claim. Covered Entity's workforce approves every action and submits every claim. The Software does not
submit claims, corrected claims or appeals to anyone.

C. These services are payment activities of Covered Entity within the meaning of 45 C.F.R. § 164.501 and
involve the use and disclosure of protected health information. The parties enter into this Agreement to
satisfy 45 C.F.R. §§ 164.308(b), 164.314(a), 164.502(e) and 164.504(e).

The parties therefore agree as follows.

## 1. Definitions

Required by Law, Secretary, Security Incident, Subcontractor, Unsecured Protected Health Information, and

1.2 **Specific definitions.**

(a) "Business Associate" has the meaning given to "business associate" at 45 C.F.R. § 160.103 and, in
reference to the party to this Agreement, means the business associate identified above.

(b) "Covered Entity" has the meaning given to "covered entity" at 45 C.F.R. § 160.103 and, in reference
to the party to this Agreement, means the covered entity identified above.

(c) "HIPAA Rules" means the Privacy, Security, Breach Notification, and Enforcement Rules at 45 C.F.R.
Parts 160 and 164.

(d) "PHI" means Protected Health Information, including electronic Protected Health Information, that
Business Associate creates, receives, maintains or transmits on behalf of Covered Entity.

(e) "Business Day" means Monday through Friday, excluding federal holidays.

(f) "Export Package" means the file the Software produces for one work item approved by Covered Entity's
workforce, containing the corrected claim, appeal letter or question for Covered Entity, a summary, and

(g) "Model Provider" means the Subcontractor listed in Exhibit B that provides the language model the
Software may consult.

(h) "Services" means the services described in Recital B and Exhibit A.

(i) "Software" has the meaning given in Recital B.

## 2. Obligations of Business Associate

Business Associate shall:

2.1 **Permitted uses only.** Not use or disclose PHI other than as permitted or required by this Agreement
or as Required by Law.

2.2 **Safeguards.** Use appropriate safeguards, and comply with Subpart C of 45 C.F.R. Part 164 with
respect to electronic PHI, to prevent use or disclosure of PHI other than as provided for by this
Agreement; maintain written security policies and procedures and a written risk analysis; and make them
available to Covered Entity on request.

2.3 **Reporting.** Report to Covered Entity any use or disclosure of PHI not provided for by this Agreement
of which it becomes aware, including Breaches of Unsecured Protected Health Information as required by
45 C.F.R. § 164.410, and any Security Incident of which it becomes aware, as follows:

(a) *Breaches of Unsecured Protected Health Information:* without unreasonable delay and in no event later
than five (5) Business Days after discovery, and in every case within the sixty (60) calendar days
permitted by 45 C.F.R. § 164.410(b), subject to any delay required by a law enforcement official under
45 C.F.R. § 164.412. A Breach is treated as discovered on the first day on which it is known to Business
Associate or, by exercising reasonable diligence, would have been known to Business Associate.

(b) *Other uses or disclosures not provided for by this Agreement:* within five (5) Business Days after
Business Associate becomes aware of them.

(c) *Security Incidents:* within five (5) Business Days for any Security Incident that results in
unauthorized access to, or use, disclosure, modification or destruction of, PHI, or in interference with
the operation of the Software, and within five (5) Business Days for any other successful Security
Incident affecting a system or account that holds or gives access to PHI. This Section 2.3(c) constitutes
notice, for which no further report is required, of the ongoing occurrence of unsuccessful Security
Incidents, such as pings and port scans, refused log-in attempts and blocked malware, that do not result
in unauthorized access to, or use, disclosure, modification or destruction of, PHI.

(d) *Content:* each report shall include, to the extent possible, the identification of each Individual
whose Unsecured Protected Health Information has been, or is reasonably believed by Business Associate to
have been, accessed, acquired, used or disclosed, together with the other information listed in Exhibit C
that is then available. Business Associate shall supplement its report promptly as further information
becomes available.

(e) *Notices to others:* Covered Entity shall determine whether, when and how to notify Individuals, the
Secretary and the media. Business Associate shall not give such notices with respect to PHI unless
Covered Entity directs it to do so in writing. Business Associate shall reimburse Covered Entity for the
reasonable, documented costs of notifications required by law that result from a Breach caused by
Business Associate or its Subcontractor. That reimbursement is direct damages, within the limit set by
Section 13.2 of the Pilot Agreement.

2.4 **Subcontractors.** In accordance with 45 C.F.R. §§ 164.502(e)(1)(ii) and 164.308(b)(2), ensure that
any Subcontractor that creates, receives, maintains or transmits PHI on behalf of Business Associate
agrees in writing to the same restrictions, conditions and requirements that apply to Business Associate
with respect to such information. The Subcontractors permitted under this Agreement are those listed in
Exhibit B:

(a) the Model Provider, to which Business Associate shall not disclose PHI until (i) a business associate
agreement between Business Associate and the Model Provider is in effect, (ii) the conditions listed in
Exhibit B are met, and (iii) Business Associate has given Covered Entity written notice; and

(b) the secure transfer service and the video service, if the Pilot Agreement Cover Page shows that
Business Associate provides them, once each has signed a business associate agreement with Business
Associate and keeps PHI in the United States. That selection on the Cover Page is Covered Entity's prior
written consent to the service as a Subcontractor.

Business Associate shall not engage any other Subcontractor without Covered Entity's prior written
consent.

2.5 **Access.** Make available PHI in a Designated Record Set to Covered Entity as necessary to satisfy
Covered Entity's obligations under 45 C.F.R. § 164.524, within ten (10) Business Days after Covered
Entity's written request. Business Associate shall forward to Covered Entity, within five (5) Business
Days, any request for access that it receives directly from an Individual, and shall not respond to the
Individual itself.

2.6 **Amendment.** Make any amendment(s) to PHI in a Designated Record Set as directed or agreed to by
Covered Entity pursuant to 45 C.F.R. § 164.526, or take other measures as necessary to satisfy Covered
Entity's obligations under 45 C.F.R. § 164.526, within ten (10) Business Days after Covered Entity's
written direction, and forward to Covered Entity within five (5) Business Days any request for amendment
that it receives directly from an Individual.

2.7 **Accounting.** Maintain and make available the information required to provide an accounting of
disclosures to Covered Entity as necessary to satisfy Covered Entity's obligations under 45 C.F.R.
§ 164.528, within ten (10) Business Days after Covered Entity's written request, and forward to Covered
Entity within five (5) Business Days any request for an accounting that it receives directly from an
Individual.

2.8 **Privacy Rule obligations.** To the extent Business Associate carries out one or more of Covered
Entity's obligations under Subpart E of 45 C.F.R. Part 164, comply with the requirements of Subpart E that
apply to Covered Entity in the performance of such obligations.

2.9 **Books and records.** Make its internal practices, books and records available to the Secretary for
purposes of determining compliance with the HIPAA Rules.

2.10 **No submission.** Not submit any claim, corrected claim, appeal or other request to any payer or
clearinghouse, and not correspond with payers or patients on Covered Entity's behalf. Covered Entity's
workforce decides whether to submit the contents of each Export Package and does so through Covered
Entity's own channels.

2.11 **United States only.** Not access, use, store, transmit or otherwise process PHI outside the United
States, and not permit any Subcontractor to do so. For the Model Provider, this includes the
configuration described in Exhibit B that restricts processing and storage to the United States.

2.12 **No sale, marketing or model training.** Not sell PHI, not use or disclose PHI for marketing, and not
use PHI to train or fine-tune any machine-learning model or permit the Model Provider to do so.

2.13 **Other artificial-intelligence services.** Not enter PHI into any artificial-intelligence tool,
application or service other than the Model Provider account described in Exhibit B, including consumer
chat assistants and coding assistants.

2.14 **No PHI in the Software.** Not place PHI in the Software's source code, rules, prompts, templates,
test data or documentation. Business Associate may adjust the Software's rules, prompts and templates in
response to what the Services show for Covered Entity, using only synthetic data to test such changes.

2.15 **Workforce.** Limit access to PHI to members of its workforce who need it to perform the Services,
each with a unique account, each trained before receiving access and at least annually, and each
protected by multi-factor authentication for the Software and for every online service that holds PHI,
and by a password together with the BitLocker start-up PIN on the workstation account. Business Associate
shall notify Covered Entity in writing before any person other than Connor Betz is given access to PHI.

2.16 **Mitigation and cooperation.** Mitigate, to the extent practicable, any harmful effect known to
Business Associate of a use or disclosure of PHI by Business Associate or its Subcontractor in violation
of this Agreement, and cooperate with Covered Entity's investigation and risk assessment of any Breach or
Security Incident.

2.17 **Audit records.** Make available to Covered Entity on request the Software's audit records for
Covered Entity's data. The Software keeps an append-only, hash-chained audit log of the imports,
processing steps, model requests, item views, review actions and log-ins performed through it; Business
Associate records other access to PHI in its access and incident logs. The Software's anonymize and
deanonymize commands, which Covered Entity runs on its own computer, record no audit events; each
completed run is recorded in a run log in Covered Entity's key folder, and each import of a
pseudonymized batch is audited like every import.

## 3. Permitted Uses and Disclosures

3.1 **Services.** Business Associate may use or disclose PHI only as necessary to perform the following
Services for Covered Entity:

(a) receiving Covered Entity's remittance files, claim data, clinical note excerpts and payer
correspondence within the scope set out in Exhibit A;

(b) matching remittances to claims, identifying denied claims and classifying each denial;

(c) proposing corrected claims, appeals or questions, drafting appeal letters, and preparing Export
Packages for review, approval and submission by Covered Entity's workforce;

(d) tracking the outcome of each claim that Covered Entity's workforce records as submitted, using later
remittance files;

(e) measuring the accuracy and results of the Services and reporting them to Covered Entity;

(f) disclosing PHI to the Model Provider, as a Subcontractor, solely through the Software's redaction
safeguard and only in accordance with Section 2.4. Before each request, the safeguard replaces with
substitute tokens the identifiers recorded for the claim: the patient's name, member identifier, account
number, address and date of birth; the claim numbers assigned by Covered Entity and by the payer, and the
line control numbers; the names of the rendering provider and of the note authors; and the dates recorded
on the claim, remittance, clinical notes and payer correspondence. It also masks Social Security numbers
and telephone numbers written with the usual separators, e-mail addresses, and dates labeled as a date of
birth, and it blocks any request in which it detects a remaining identifier of these kinds. Other dates,
names of other persons, health plan group numbers, prior authorization and payment trace numbers, and
numbers written without separators can remain in a request;

(g) returning PHI to Covered Entity, including Export Packages and the hand-over copy described in
Section 5.3; and

(h) creating information that is de-identified in accordance with 45 C.F.R. § 164.514(a) through (c),
solely to report and describe aggregate results of the pilot (counts, rates, review times and dollar
totals) and to improve the Software, as permitted by the Pilot Agreement. Business Associate shall not
create or retain any record-level de-identified data set.

3.2 **Required by law.** Business Associate may use or disclose PHI as Required by Law. Business Associate
shall notify Covered Entity in writing within five (5) Business Days after receiving a subpoena, court
order or other demand for PHI, and before disclosing, unless the law prohibits notice; shall disclose only
the PHI the demand requires; and shall record each such disclosure for the accounting described in
Section 2.7.

3.3 **Minimum necessary.** Business Associate shall make uses, disclosures and requests of PHI subject to
the following minimum necessary requirements:

(a) requests are limited to the information listed in Exhibit A: denied claims only, within the date range
stated in the Pilot Agreement, with the claim fields the Software needs, and clinical note excerpts only
for denied claims in scope, limited to the denied visit and, for a medical-necessity denial, the initial
examination or the latest re-examination;

(b) the Model Provider receives only the tokenized context that the Software builds for one denial at a
time; and

(c) Business Associate's communications with Covered Entity identify items by work item number rather
than by patient name.

3.4 **Subpart E.** Business Associate shall not use or disclose PHI in a manner that would violate
Subpart E of 45 C.F.R. Part 164 if done by Covered Entity, except as permitted by Section 3.5.

3.5 **Management and administration.** Business Associate may use PHI for its proper management and
administration and to carry out its legal responsibilities, and may disclose PHI for those purposes if the
disclosure is Required by Law or Business Associate obtains the reasonable assurances described in
45 C.F.R. § 164.504(e)(4)(ii)(B), in each case limited to the minimum necessary.

## 5. Term and Termination

5.1 **Term.** This Agreement is effective as of the Effective Date and continues until the Pilot Agreement
ends and Business Associate has completed Section 5.3, unless terminated earlier under Section 5.2.

5.2 **Termination for cause.** Business Associate authorizes termination of this Agreement by Covered
Entity if Covered Entity determines that Business Associate has violated a material term of this
Agreement and Business Associate has not cured the breach or ended the violation within thirty (30) days
after written notice from Covered Entity, or immediately if cure is not possible. Termination of this
Agreement also terminates the Pilot Agreement.

5.3 **Obligations upon termination.** When the Pilot Agreement ends or this Agreement is terminated,
whichever occurs first, Business Associate shall return or destroy all PHI received from Covered Entity,
or created, maintained or received by Business Associate on behalf of Covered Entity, that Business
Associate still maintains in any form, returning the hand-over copy described in Section 5.3(b) and
destroying all other PHI, and shall retain no copies. Within thirty (30) days after that date, Business
Associate shall:

(a) stop processing Covered Entity's data;

(b) run the Software's clinic purge, which deletes every uploaded file and Export Package stored by the
Software; writes Covered Entity's patient, claim, clinical note, payer correspondence, work item, proposal,
review and outcome records, and any unmatched remittance records, into a single file (the "hand-over
copy"); deletes those records, except that remittance records remain in the database without patient
names or member identifiers until the database file is destroyed under Section 5.3(d); and records the
deletion in the Software's audit log;

(c) deliver the hand-over copy to Covered Entity through the secure transfer method specified in the Pilot
Agreement, together with its record of disclosures subject to 45 C.F.R. § 164.528 and, on request, the
Software's audit records for Covered Entity's data, and delete the hand-over copy after Covered Entity
confirms receipt, or at once if Covered Entity declines it. If Covered Entity neither confirms receipt nor
declines within ten (10) Business Days after delivery, Business Associate shall remind it in writing; if
Covered Entity has not responded within ten (10) Business Days after the reminder, it is treated as having
declined the hand-over copy. Until then Business Associate keeps the hand-over copy encrypted and does not
use it, and the thirty-day period is extended by the time spent waiting;

(d) destroy all other copies of PHI held by Business Associate or by its Subcontractors on its behalf,
including the remaining database file, files received from Covered Entity, files on a secure transfer
service Business Associate provides, downloaded copies and backups; and

(e) deliver to Covered Entity a written certification of return and destruction, signed by Business
Associate, in the form attached to the Pilot Agreement.

5.4 **Infeasibility.** If return or destruction of any PHI is not feasible, including PHI that the Model
Provider must retain under law or under its business associate agreement, and records that Business
Associate must keep to carry out its legal responsibilities under Section 3.5 (such as fee statements and
compliance records that identify items by work item number), Business Associate shall describe that PHI
in its certification, extend the protections of this Agreement to it, and limit further uses and
disclosures to those purposes that make return or destruction infeasible, for so long as it is
maintained. Business Associate shall ensure that the Model Provider returns or destroys PHI as its
business associate agreement with Business Associate requires.

5.5 **Survival.** The obligations of Business Associate under this Section 5 survive the termination of this
Agreement.
